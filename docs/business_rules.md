# Dental Office Database Business Rules

 A patient may have zero or many appointments. Each appointment must belong to one patient.
 A dentist may have zero or many appointments. Each appointment must be assigned to one dentist.
 An appointment may have one or more treatments. Each treatment must belong to one appointment.
 A service may be used for zero or many treatments. Each treatment must be associated with one appointment.
 A patients may have zero or many payments. Each payment must belong to one patient.
 A patient may have zero or one insurance record. Each insurance record must belong to one patient.
 An employee may manage zero or many appointments. Each appointment may be managed by one employee.
 A dentist may perform zero or many treatments. Each treatment must be performed by one dentist.
